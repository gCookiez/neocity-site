What is Kamen Rider?

Kamen rider is a long-running TV tokusatsu series that airs in Japan. It features a protagonist who is able to transform with a power suit that unleashes unrivaled power. Their power source are typically derived from the evil organization's research but the protagonist refuse fealty to the very organization itself. The first rider had this formula setting where an evil organization called "SHOCKER" kidnaps a young professor, Takeshi Hongo, and converts him into a cyborg in hopes to conquer the whole world for SHOCKER. Fortunately, the professor is resistant to brainwashing, resulting him to immediately to defect from the organization and side to Justice. He now serves as a warrior to fight against the organization.

Years goes by, many variations of rider have been created to reality that invoke different themes and motifs. Examples being a rider that has a time-travelling train, a warrior that can summon their monster, a video game doctor, even a fruit samurai!

This year's rider (as of this page's publishing) is about a secret agent in the dreams and he fights nightmares using his dream learning skill. His rider name is ZEZTZ and he protects the dreams of everyone! Thus, this page's theme. As you can see, the background is based on the real light show of the DX ZEZTZ driver. More about drivers, drivers are the tools that let's the user transform into their power suit, manifesting the given powers. Usually enhances strength, speed, agility, and many more!

Where to start?

As per suggestion to many fans of tokusatsu, you have a free choice on what series to pick first. As a guideline, look up what the main rider looks like for the series and pick the one that looks cool to you!

If you are interested on the gimmicks that the shows offer, I'll introduce some of the series:

  • Kamen Rider Gaim - Lockseeds are fruit based lockets that are sources of power for their armor.
  • Kamen Rider Ex-Aid - Utilizing the power of video game cartridges to powerup the users.
  • Kamen Rider Drive- The power of racecars to speed up their surroundings against slowdowns.

If you are story oriented, here are also some suggestions:
  • Kamen Rider Saber - A novelist who is interested on creating stories was introduced to a clan of swordsmen who fight against evil story monsters.
  • Kamen Rider Hibiki - a middle school teenager meets a mystrious man who will mentor the kid for self discovery. The mentor also happens to be an oni who uses the power of sound to exterminate them.
  • Kamen Rider Build - An astronaut found a mysterious box and brought it back to earth. Upon return, the astronaut was suddenly compelled to activate the box and spawned walls that segregate Japan into three nations. Years later, an insomniac physicist harnesses the power of Build. He is also met with a convict fighter who is accused to the murder of a civilian. Will they ever solve the mysteries of their dillema?


The rest of the series is up to your curiousity and discovery. Just don't get lost too much on any of the choices!

Gimmick - Lockseeds

Lockseeds are artifacts that are generated while wearing the Sengoku driver and picking the hellheim fruits. The Hellheim fruits are potent food specimens from an alternate dimension. These are not for consumption as consuming one will turn an unsuspecting person into an Inves. An Inves is a naturally spawning monster in that dimension. These specimens crave the fruits that are spawning in the forest.
